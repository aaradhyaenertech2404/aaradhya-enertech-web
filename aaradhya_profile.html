About Aaradhya Enertech

Powering Progress with Precision Engineering

With over a decade and a half of manufacturing leadership, Aaradhya Enertech is recognized as one of Western India's premier transformer manufacturers, driving reliable energy distribution across government DISCOMs, EPC projects, and global industrial parks.

🎯 Our Vision

To be India's most trusted innovator in smart power transformation, reducing transmission losses and facilitating carbon-neutral energy integration through state-of-the-art electromagnetic design.

⚙️ Our Mission

Manufacture zero-defect, BIS-certified, and BEE Star energy-rated distribution equipment backed by rigorous lifetime thermal endurance testing and round-the-clock field service support.

Factory Infrastructure & Quality Control
Manufacturing Process

Zero-Defect Production & Testing Facility

Our Bindayaka, Jaipur manufacturing complex integrates high-precision core cutting, computer-controlled coil winding, vacuum drying ovens, and an in-house computerized testing laboratory.

01
CRGO Core Stacking
Step-lap mitered core joints minimize magnetizing currents and harmonic vibration.
02
Precision Winding
Continuous disc and helical copper coils wound with Nomex / Kraft paper insulation.
03
Core-Coil Assembly
Rigid clamping prevents movement during short circuits and mechanical shocks.
04
Vacuum Oven Drying
Moisture extracted under high thermal vacuum before immediate oil filling.
05
Tanking & Polyurethane Coat
Shot-blasted MS steel tanks with corrosion-resistant epoxy primers and topcoats.
06
Routine & Type Testing
100% routine tests as per IS/IEC standards prior to factory dispatch release.
Comprehensive Test Routine Conducted In-House:
• Measurement of Winding Resistance & Turns Ratio
• Measurement of No-Load Loss and Current
• Separate Source AC Withstand Voltage Test
• Induced Over-Voltage Withstand Test (DVDF)
• Oil Breakdown Dielectric Test (BDV > 60 kV)
• Short Circuit & Temperature Rise Verified (NABL labs)
