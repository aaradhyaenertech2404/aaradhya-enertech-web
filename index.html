OUR TRANSFORMERS

Aaradhya Enertech Industries has been dedicated to manufacturing high-performance power equipment and precision transformer components. Our transformer division produces power and distribution transformers, inverter duty transformers for solar and wind applications, and custom furnace transformers—all engineered up to 20 MVA and 33 kV class.

Operating comprehensive in-house manufacturing, automated CNC slitting, and step-lap core stacking facilities in Jaipur, Rajasthan, we supply leading EPC contractors, state power utilities, and renewable energy developers across India.

Our transformer core components—processed to stringent standards from prime-grade CRGO electrical steel—deliver ultra-low watt loss, minimal magnetostriction noise, and long-term operational reliability across demanding grid conditions.
