## 2. Logo Variants & Recommended Applications

### 1. `logo-full-light.png` (Vertical Lockup - Light Backgrounds)
- **Description:** Stylized "A" symbol + Dark Navy typography + Green tagline on a transparent background.
- **Recommended Usage:** 
  - Official company letterheads & quotation PDFs
  - White website navigation header
  - Company envelopes, invoices, and purchase orders

### 2. `logo-full-dark.png` (Vertical Lockup - Dark Backgrounds)
- **Description:** Stylized "A" symbol + Brilliant White typography + Bright Emerald tagline on a transparent background.
- **Recommended Usage:**
  - Dark website footers
  - Factory outdoor entrance signboards
  - Employee uniforms, safety jackets, and polo shirts
  - Dark-mode digital presentations

### 3. `logo-icon.png` (Symbol Mark Only - 600×600)
- **Description:** The iconic stylized "A" energy mark centered on a square transparent canvas.
- **Recommended Usage:**
  - WhatsApp Business Profile Picture
  - Social media avatars (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram)
  - Factory equipment labels & product packaging stickers

### 4. Browser Favicon Suite (`favicon.ico`, `favicon-32x32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, etc.)
- **Description:** Multi-resolution pixel-perfect icons formatted specifically for web browsers, mobile home screens, and browser bookmarks.
